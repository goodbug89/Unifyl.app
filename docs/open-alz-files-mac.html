How to open ALZ files on a Mac

Updated 2026 · for anyone who just received a .alz from Korea

ALZ is a Korean archive format created by the ALZip utility — still everywhere in Korean business, government, and older download archives. macOS's built-in Archive Utility doesn't know it: double-click a .alz and you get "unable to expand".

Your options

  • Bandizip for Mac — free extractor that handles ALZ. Fine if extraction is all you need.
  • The Unarchiver — the veteran free tool; ALZ support is hit-or-miss depending on the file.
  • Unifyl (this site) — opens the ALZ like a folder inside a file manager, so you can browse it and copy out just the file you need. Free; details below.

Better than extracting: browse it like a folder

Most ALZ files arrive with one or two files you actually want. In Unifyl, press Return on the archive and you're inside it — a normal folder listing. Put the cursor on the file you need, press F5, and it lands in the opposite pane. No full extraction, no temp-folder cleanup. The same gesture works for ZIP, 7z, TAR and RAR.

  1. Download Unifyl (free) and launch it.
  2. Navigate to the .alz file and press Return.
  3. Select what you need and press F5 to copy it out.

Note: ALZ support ships in the direct download from unifyl.app. The Mac App Store edition can't bundle the ALZ engine due to sandbox rules — if ALZ is why you're here, grab the direct build. It's free either way.
